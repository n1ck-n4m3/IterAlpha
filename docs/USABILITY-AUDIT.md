# IterAlpha usability audit

Date: 2026-10-24

This is a **synthetic, hypothesis-generating review**, not a study with 100 human users. The 100 profiles are the Cartesian product of four experience levels, five tasks, and five language contexts. Half use a mobile viewport. The reproducible list is generated by `scripts/persona-audit.mjs` into `docs/persona-audit.json`. Counts indicate how many constructed profiles encounter a design constraint, not a population prevalence or conversion estimate.

## Representative market patterns

- [Coinbase Advanced](https://help.coinbase.com/en-ca/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/dashboard-overview) exposes charts, order books, volume, indicators, and persistent chart settings. Its breadth is useful for an experienced trader, but IterAlpha should keep the first decision simpler.
- [Kraken Pro](https://support.kraken.com/articles/kraken-pro-trading-interface-guide?mode=consumerapp) offers customizable TradingView-style charts and chart-based order adjustment. IterAlpha can borrow the discoverability of chart controls without implying that its simulation places exchange orders.
- [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000502344-moving-average-convergence-divergence-macd-indicator/) places the MACD line, signal, and histogram in a separate pane. Its [moving-average guide](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000696841-simple-moving-average/) explicitly describes MAs as lagging indicators. This is the right mental model to teach beginners.
- [Kraken Learn](https://www.kraken.com/learn/trading) separates beginner and deeper trading material. IterAlpha's own two tracks should similarly vary the level of explanation while retaining one decision loop.

## Decisions from the review

1. **Make indicators visible, but explain them.** The chart now has independently toggleable MA 7, MA 25, volume, MACD, and RSI. Volume and momentum have their own panes. Foundations shows short explanations; a control cannot silently become a buy/sell instruction.
2. **Do not reveal future bars through a calculation.** The same cutoff used for the training chart feeds the indicator functions. A regression test compares every past indicator value before and after more bars are revealed.
3. **Reduce repeat setup.** Indicator choices persist in this browser, matching the low-click preference seen in professional charting tools.
4. **Offer language choice where decisions happen.** Eleven interface languages are selectable and saved. The language control is visible on both the home page and training page. Translation is currently limited to primary navigation, chart and indicator controls, and key decision actions. Scenario evidence, authored lessons, and coach feedback remain English and are labeled as such when another language is selected.
5. **Keep simulated data honest.** The current Market Lab and training paths remain labeled synthetic. A real historical/news replay requires licensed, timestamped source data; a generated headline must never be presented as past market news.

## Open issues for the next iteration

- Translate the complete training corpus, review text, errors, account UI, and accessibility descriptions with a reviewed glossary. The present language selector is a beta interface layer, not complete localization.
- Test chart density and information bubbles with actual mobile users and screen readers. The 100 profiles cannot validate comprehension or interaction speed.
- Add selectable MA parameters and chart presets after observing real traders; indicator proliferation could hide the decision task.
- Run moderated tests with new and experienced traders. Measure time to first decision, order-size comprehension, indicator interpretation, and whether feedback changes a later decision.
